Foundation

Block Viewer stands on the open-source Block Viewer codebase as its foundation. We keep that engine, protocol work, and GPL lineage, and build on top of it for a different audience — Block’s look, tools, and product direction — rather than shipping Aurora under another name.

What we keep

GPL-compatible lineage, protocol expectations, and the hard-won viewer engineering Aurora published as open source. Full credit lives in NOTICE.md.

What we change

Product name, branding, UI chrome, packaging, and the roadmap aimed at Block users — not a drop-in Aurora rebadge.

Not affiliated

Not an official Block Viewer release. Not affiliated with Linden Lab or Second Life.

Sister project

Block (GPPI) is the post-processing injector for other viewers. Separate repository — do not conflate downloads or issues.
